I'm a BBA Marketing graduate from Sam Houston State University (May 2026) and a former NCAA Division I tennis athlete — four years competing at No. 1 singles. That combination shaped how I think: data-driven, competitive, and always tuned to what moves people.

I speak four languages (English, Tamil, Kannada, Hindi), which means I understand brand voice across cultures — not just markets. I'm drawn to the intersection of sports, lifestyle, and identity: where brands stop selling products and start building belonging.

Currently based in Bangalore. Eyes on New York.

005 — My Story
Grew up between Bangalore courts and Texas campuses. Sport taught me everything I know about pressure, strategy, and showing up.

I was raised in Bangalore in a household where curiosity was the default mode. Tennis became my first serious education — it demanded discipline, quick adaptation, and knowing when to attack and when to hold. Those instincts translate directly into how I approach campaigns and brand challenges.

Competing at Division I level while earning Academic All-WAC honors twice taught me something simple: the best performers prepare obsessively and stay calm under pressure. I bring that to every brief, every pitch, every deadline. Marketing, to me, is a sport you never stop practicing.
