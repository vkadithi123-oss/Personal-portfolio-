UX Meets Brand
Good UX and good branding are the same thing — making people feel understood.

I approach UX through a marketer's lens. Every interaction a user has with a brand is a brand moment — and it should feel intentional, consistent, and human.

Google's UX Design certification gave me the formal framework. My background in brand marketing gives me the strategic lens. Together, they mean I design for both the user and the brand simultaneously.
